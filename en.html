What the system controls

The four areas from your request

01

Tenders

  • Every morning it searches for tenders matching your profile: keywords, regions, minimum value
  • Selects the relevant ones and shows the price and submission deadline
  • Assigns a responsible person and sends reminders 3 and 1 working day before the submission deadline
  • We connect platforms based on your list of countries; paid platform plans are covered by you
02

Stock

  • Stock levels of ingredients, cultures, enzymes and cleaning agents
  • Receipts and issues come from a spreadsheet, from your accounting software or are entered with two taps in the bot (Optimal package)
  • Batch shelf lives: alerts 60 and 30 days ahead on what to sell or use first
  • Alerts when an item drops below the minimum
  • Prepares a draft order to the supplier, sent on your command
  • If you keep records in 1C, we will confirm the price after reviewing the database
03

Deadlines and emails

  • Deadline register: product certificates and registrations by country, permits, contract end dates, payments
  • Reminds 14, 7, 3 and 1 calendar day before and brings in the manager when a deadline is missed
  • Reading incoming emails and finding deadlines in them is available in the Maximum package: AI can miss an email, so the register remains the main tool and the mailbox serves as a safety net
04

Report to the owner

  • All three areas in one place: what is in progress, what is stuck, what is overdue and with whom
  • At any time, without calling staff or going through spreadsheets
  • PDF for printing or forwarding

Data and security

Your data stays yours

How it works, to be set out in the contract

  • Role-based access: staff see only their own tasks, the owner sees everything
  • The system reads only the mailboxes and spreadsheets you have chosen
  • AI suggests, a person confirms. Emails are never sent automatically
  • We do not use your data in work for other clients
  • Your data, settings and access rights remain yours, including if you end the support. The studio's shared modules are used under licence

What we will discuss before starting

  • Data is processed on servers in the EU. We will sign a data processing agreement (DPA under Art. 28 GDPR) and show you the list of all subcontractors the data passes through. We will agree with you which AI service to use before launch; mailboxes containing personal data can be handled without AI
  • Which emails and contracts contain personal data, and whether they may be passed to AI
  • Where to store the data: on your side or on a separate server
  • What to do with correspondence that AI must not read
